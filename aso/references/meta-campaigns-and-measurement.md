# Meta Campaigns and Measurement

Meta's own rules for iOS app campaigns, from Meta's help and developer documentation captured November 9, 2026: campaign setup for iOS 14.5+, optimization goals and their event requirements, the learning phase, bids, limits, attribution and what Ads Manager reports. Help pages are undated and count as current; developer pages carry an "Updated" date, and where a dated developer page and a current help page differ, follow the help page and treat the developer statement as history. Meta's pages disagree on several limits and names; both sides are given as (contested) with dates, and several changes roll out per account, so check what the account shows before applying a rule. Meta documents mechanics, not outcomes: creative, ad-to-page alignment and App Store Connect are in `references/meta-ads.md`, consent and the ATT prompt in `references/app-tracking-transparency.md`, App Store Connect metric definitions in `references/measurement-and-cadence.md`.

## Campaign types and setup for iOS 14.5+

- Pick the objective by goal (Meta-documented): app promotion finds new users likely to download; engagement, leads, sales and traffic reengage existing users toward an in-app action; playable ads let people try an interactive preview first. App promotion was called app installs; only the name changed.
- Meta's example sequence for a new app: optimize for installs, then for app events or maximize value once in-app actions or value matter (Meta-documented). Revisit the objective as goals change (Meta recommends).
- Advantage+ app campaigns (formerly automated app ads) are Meta's app promotion product: Meta AI sets bidding, audiences and placements, manual adjustments remain possible, and they now sit with Advantage+ catalog ads in Ads Manager (Meta-documented). They support only app promotion, never traffic, engagement, leads or sales (Meta-documented).
- App promotion campaigns reaching iOS 14.5 or later are "iOS 14+ campaigns". One help page says only Advantage+ setup is available for them, each has one ad set with a fixed optimization choice and one optimization type, and buying is auction only (Meta-documented). Android and iOS 13.7-or-earlier campaigns keep standard limits and can share the ad account (Meta-documented).
- Meta also suggests split-testing a constrained manual setup against Advantage+ (Meta recommends), which sits badly with "Advantage+ only" for iOS 14.5+; run that comparison on Android or older iOS if at all (inference).
- Prerequisites (Meta-documented): the app registered on Meta for Developers and an ad account linked to it; without either, ads run but optimize for link clicks and track no installs. Optimizing for or measuring installs, events or value needs app events, which current help pages accept from the Facebook SDK, a measurement partner integration or the Conversions API. The objective page and a developer page updated 2021-08-16 still name the SDK alone; read that as dated.
- Install optimization needs at least one install in the last 28 days; with none, the campaign optimizes for link clicks until one arrives (Meta-documented).
- Targeting in an Advantage+ app campaign (Meta-documented): country (required, or regions or worldwide), optional language, and operating system. No age, gender, device, relationship, interest, behavior, lookalike or custom audience targeting. Minimum age (18+ by default, 21 in some countries) and city or region locations are account-wide Advertising settings; custom audience exclusions and attribution settings are per app. The third input is (contested): the benefits list says app store, the targeting page and the unsupported-features list say operating system, adding that the app store selection guides device relevance.
- Anyone who installed or opened the app in the last 90 days is ineligible for that app's Advantage+ ads (Meta-documented).
- Placements are always Advantage+ placements, as many as possible across Facebook, Messenger, Instagram and Audience Network; they cannot be chosen (Meta-documented).
- Structure (Meta recommends): no simultaneous Advantage+ app campaigns to the same audience, never one country in several campaigns, group by geography, product category, language or average revenue per user, and tier countries by past performance. Ads Manager blocks duplicate setups but cannot filter for Advantage+ app campaigns, so give them memorable names (Meta-documented).
- Run app ads when the app ships new content, such as a feature or a sale (Meta recommends).
- Teens: Meta paused ads to teens in the EU, the EEA and Switzerland from the week of November 6, 2023, described as temporary; no later status is documented (Meta-documented, 2023).

## Optimization goals and event requirements

- Advantage+ app campaigns have four performance goals (Meta-documented): maximize app installs (broad targeting, KPI cost per install), maximize link clicks (cost per link click), maximize app events (cost per event or return on ad spend) and maximize value of conversions (return on ad spend). Value allows lowest cost bidding only and Purchase as the only event; Meta's table lost its footnote markers, so tying both limits to this goal is inferred.
- Choose the goal by the primary business goal; test goals by changing only the goal, keeping country, language and operating system the same (Meta recommends).
- Installs give reach and the lowest cost per new install; link click optimization is not recommended and is a fallback for small advertisers without the SDK (Meta recommends, developer page updated 2024-10-23).
- App event optimization delivers to people likely to complete the chosen event, so it buys installs from people likely to act, and it bills on impressions like install optimization (Meta-documented). It requires the Facebook SDK (8.0 or later in iOS 14+ campaigns) or a connected measurement partner, the events set up, and an app eligible for AEM or SKAdNetwork; under SKAdNetwork attribution the event must be in the SKAdNetwork configuration before it can be selected (Meta-documented).
- Optimizable standard events (contested count): 15 on a developer page updated 2024-10-23 (Achieve Level, Add Payment Info, Add to Cart, Add to Wishlist, Complete Registration, Content View, Initiate Checkout, Purchase, Rate, Search, Spend Credits, Start Trial, Subscribe, Tutorial Completion, Unlocked Achievement), 14 on a current help page. Not every standard event qualifies, and an ad set optimizes for one event at a time (Meta-documented). For subscription apps the candidates are Start Trial and Subscribe, with currency, predicted_ltv and value parameters (Meta-documented, 2024).
- Custom event optimization is (contested): two help pages say custom events can be optimized in app promotion, and only there, though not for value; the "About app ads" page says they can be created but not optimized on; the AEM help page says AEM for app events does not support custom event optimization. For an iOS campaign on AEM, plan on a standard event (inference). Where a custom event is matched to a standard one, Meta recommends optimizing for the custom event.
- Pick the event this way (Meta recommends): tie it to a business KPI; prefer a fitting standard event to a new custom one; require more than 100 attributed conversions in the two weeks before launch, checked in Ads Manager (attributed only), not Events Manager; prefer a mid- or deep-funnel event completed by 1% to 30% of users, such as a first purchase or a paid subscription; set events up a few days early, since new or edited events take up to 72 hours to become available. Send all conversion events from campaigns on event optimization.
- Maximize value of conversions seeks the highest total value, not the most conversions. In app promotion it works on purchase values only; every other value type, predicted lifetime value (pLTV) and profit included, is web-only under the Sales objective (Meta-documented). Meta's example: $200 bought 4 purchases worth $250 on count optimization (ROAS 1.25) and 3 worth $500 on value optimization (ROAS 2.5). On a SKAdNetwork-attributed iOS campaign, value needs purchase events with value sets and the value toggle, which takes at least 4 event slots (Meta-documented).
- Value test (Meta recommends): at least 3 weeks, budget for 50 or more conversions a week (100 or more for pLTV and profit), no ROAS goal at first, judged by an A/B test or lift study, since peak performance typically comes after about 2 weeks. Expect higher cost per result and fewer conversions during learning, which usually lasts at least a week (Meta-documented).

## The learning phase and volume floors

- Learning starts when an ad or ad set is created or significantly edited; ad sets usually exit after about 50 results in the week after the last significant edit (Meta-documented). Until then delivery is less stable, cost per action is usually higher and results do not predict later performance, so never judge a goal, bid or creative on learning-phase numbers.
- Edits to the ad, ad set or campaign, frequent budget changes included, can send an ad set back into learning; an ad set without enough results shows "Learning limited" (Meta-documented).
- During learning (Meta recommends): wait before editing, edit only when the change should improve performance, run fewer ads and ad sets by combining similar ones, budget for enough results, and do not avoid learning altogether, since new creative needs it.
- Reporting floor: a campaign with fewer than 88 app installs a day will likely show null conversions (Meta-documented). Combine ad sets and campaigns, raise budgets or optimize for a more frequent event.

## Budgets and bid strategies

- Current names on the Advantage+ bid types page (Meta-documented): Highest volume (maximize results from the budget, no cost target), Cost per result goal (keep costs around a set amount, not guaranteed) and Bid cap (a maximum bid across auctions, for advertisers who can calculate it from predicted conversion rates). Other current pages keep the older names (contested naming): lowest cost with an optional bid cap, and cost cap, with cost cap ad sets scheduled for at least three days. Value optimization allows lowest cost only.
- A developer page updated 2023-10-05 said iOS 14+ ad sets and campaign budget optimization campaigns could not use cost cap (TARGET_COST) and that cost-per-action billing on installs was unavailable; the current help page allows cost cap ad sets, so read the 2023 restriction as superseded for cost cap (Meta-documented).
- Default to Highest volume without a bid cap: a cap controls cost but may limit results, and campaigns usually get more results and spend fully without one (Meta recommends).
- Meta documents no minimum budget. Budget for about 50 results a week, change it rarely, and aim for 88 or more installs a day per campaign (Meta recommends).
- Advantage+ campaign budget shifts spend across up to five ad sets toward the best opportunities (Meta-documented); it matters only where a campaign has several ad sets, since an Advantage+ app campaign has one, and "campaign budget optimization" on the 2023 page looks like its older name (inference).
- No Meta page addresses splitting budget between Meta and Apple Ads or any other channel.

## Account, campaign and creative limits

- Campaigns per app for iOS 14+ are (contested): 24 per app on the current help page, which also says "Updating limit" means the app ID hit its limit of 18 manual campaigns and "Pending limit" holds new campaigns until one is turned off; 9 campaigns across up to 9 ad accounts, 5 ad sets per app ID and 8 AEM conversion events on a developer page updated 2023-10-05. Advantage+ app campaigns are unlimited in number but subject to the SKAdNetwork and AEM campaign limits (Meta-documented). Plan against 24 as the current ceiling and 18 for manual campaigns; no current help page names an AEM event cap (inference).
- Locks (Meta-documented, 2023-10-05): the app's bundle ID and App Store IDs cannot change while any iOS 14+ campaign runs; a live SKAdNetwork campaign's promoted app and SKAdNetwork flag cannot be edited; an app already promoted in another ad account could not get a SKAdNetwork campaign. Settle app identifiers before launch.
- An app can log at most 1,000 distinct event names, and events cannot be removed (Meta-documented, 2021-06-17). Name events deliberately.
- New business portfolios and ad accounts may lack some features until they meet Meta's requirements (Meta-documented).
- Creative in Advantage+ app campaigns (Meta-documented): no total limit; up to 50 images, videos, playable ads or Instant Experiences per upload, 5 primary texts and 5 headlines, bulk creation of up to 50 ads. Volume advice is (contested): upload the maximum and refresh 2 to 4 times a month, never during learning, against an undated iOS 14+ page suggesting up to six options per ad set, tested first on Android or older iOS (Meta recommends, both).

## iOS attribution: AEM, SKAdNetwork and the tracking flag

- Each iOS 14+ app promotion campaign uses one attribution method: Meta's Aggregated Event Measurement (AEM) or Apple's SKAdNetwork (Meta-documented). AEM measures iOS 14+ users, opted-out users included, by removing identifiers, adding differential privacy and aggregating (Meta-documented).
- AEM now covers app promotion (Meta-documented): campaigns run without configuring app events for SKAdNetwork, more events become available for optimization, reporting is near real time, and app event or value optimization gets 1-day and 7-day click reporting, the 7-day click window and Audience Network delivery. The change rolls out per account and some accounts may not have it as of November 2026; the app must pass Meta's AEM eligibility check. This supersedes the older rule that iOS 14+ app promotion must configure SKAdNetwork events.
- SKAdNetwork is optional for accounts with the change (Meta-documented): configure its events only for a cross-platform view, and not at all when the goal is installs only. An AEM campaign gets no SKAdNetwork reporting unless SKAdNetwork is configured; then Compare attribution settings shows both, which may differ.
- Meta's SKAdNetwork pages cover 3.0 and 4.0 only (Meta-documented): 4.0 applies when the ad runs on iOS 16.1+ in Meta apps built with iOS SDK 16.1+, needs Facebook SDK 16.2.1 or later, and allows up to 63 fine-value events and 3 coarse values per postback window; a version switch takes at least 24 hours and hurts performance if repeated. The pages never mention AdAttributionKit, Apple's current name for the framework, and still call 4.0 support gradual, which looks stale.
- Advertiser Tracking Enabled (ATE) matters only for older integrations (Meta-documented, developer page updated 2024-05-02): with Facebook SDK for iOS 14.5 to 16.3.1, the Conversions API for app events or the App Events API, an iOS 14.5+ event not flagged as opted in may be restricted. On iOS 17+ with SDK 17.0.0 or later, the SDK reads the ATT status itself and ignores the flag.
- AEM attribution windows are click-only (Meta-documented): 1-day, 7-day or 28-day click for installs, 1-day or 7-day click for app event and value optimization; no view-through window is listed.
- Deep links and AEM (Meta-documented): AEM integration needs the app to support universal links passed to Meta's SDK (2023-08-25); a verified deep link is required for AEM sales, engagement and leads campaigns but not for iOS 14+ app promotion on AEM, with verification automatic from SDK 16.0.0. Deferred deep links in campaigns targeting iOS 14.5+ need SDK 18.0.3 or later; the 2023 page calling them unavailable is superseded. Meta links a page titled "Why using deep links for measurement is not recommended" whose content is undocumented here.
- Measurement partners (Meta-documented): since October 9, 2024 Meta sends AEM reporting to all of them, with integration times varying. Meta recommends partners send postbacks for events from every media source, organic included, each event through one channel only (SDK, partner or Conversions API), with revenue above 0.01 and an ISO 4217 currency code for value optimization.
- Under GDPR and other EU data protection rules, end-user consent is required before sending data through the Facebook SDK (Meta-documented, 2021-06-17). Prompt and consent rules: `references/app-tracking-transparency.md`.

## What Ads Manager reports and why it differs

- Results is the number of outcomes for the objective and settings, typically attributed installs for app install ads; cost is spend over results; app engagement columns count attributed events (installs, purchases, subscriptions, trials started, registrations, sessions) and their values (Meta-documented). D2 retention (opened 24 to 48 hours after install) needs a 7-day or 28-day window; D7 retention (6 to 7 days after) a 28-day window.
- Breakdowns by age, gender, age and gender, country, impression device, platform, platform and device and placement exist for Advantage+ and AEM campaigns, but SKAdNetwork-attributed Advantage+ campaigns show reach, impressions, clicks and spend without results (Meta-documented). Placement insights are (contested) between Meta pages. Creative reporting shows each image, video, primary text and headline.
- Why Meta's numbers differ from other counts (Meta-documented): Ads Manager shows attributed events only, Events Manager attributed and unattributed; measurement partner results differ mainly through different attribution windows per event; AEM and SKAdNetwork report differently, and Meta may model partial or missing AEM data, annotating such results; SKAdNetwork-attributed Advantage+ campaigns show no results; campaigns under 88 installs a day likely show null conversions; and, per the 2023 developer page, install and purchase metrics cannot be summed across iOS 14+ and other campaigns, though impressions and clicks can.
- Against App Store Connect (inference from the Apple and Meta rules): App Store Connect counts total downloads, first-time plus redownloads, by source type, with every in-app ad under App Referrer whichever network bought it; Meta counts installs attributed to its own clicks within its windows, partly modeled, and keeps recent installers out of Advantage+ delivery. Meta results will not match App Referrer downloads, and view-through users who search the brand land in App Store Connect Search but outside Meta's click-based results. Apple Ads and measurement partners also count installs on different rules; see `references/apple-ads.md`.

## Procedures

Assembled from Meta's documentation captured November 2026; check what your account shows, since some changes roll out per account.

1. Launch an iOS app promotion campaign: (1) register the app on Meta for Developers, link the ad account, and send app events through the Facebook SDK, a measurement partner or the Conversions API, or the campaign optimizes for link clicks; (2) choose the app promotion objective and an Advantage+ app campaign; (3) choose attribution: AEM after the eligibility check, SKAdNetwork only for its cross-platform view; (4) pick the goal: installs for a new app or thin event data, app events when a standard event completed by 1% to 30% of users has more than 100 attributed conversions in two weeks and was set up at least 72 hours ahead, value when purchases carry values; (5) set country, optional language and operating system, and keep minimum age, regions and exclusions in account settings; (6) bid Highest volume without a cap; (7) budget for about 50 results a week and 88 or more installs a day, never splitting one country across campaigns; (8) upload the maximum creative with formats per placement, and on the Apple side a custom product page per angle set (`references/meta-ads.md`); (9) leave it alone through learning, then read creative-level results by age and placement and refresh creative 2 to 4 times a month.
2. Reconcile Meta, a measurement partner and App Store Connect: (1) in Ads Manager, read Results as installs attributed to Meta clicks within the chosen window, partly modeled under AEM; (2) check optimization volume in Ads Manager, not Events Manager, which adds unattributed events; (3) align the partner's attribution windows with Meta's before comparing, since windows are the main documented cause of gaps; (4) never compare AEM and SKAdNetwork lines one for one, and never sum install or purchase metrics across iOS 14+ and other campaigns; (5) treat null conversions under 88 installs a day as a volume problem, not zero results; (6) in App Store Connect, read App Referrer downloads (first-time plus redownloads, all in-app networks) and the Search line as separate, larger figures. Output a table with one row per count: its source, definition, window and value, and a note on each gap's cause.

## Dated changes

- 2021 (developer pages): 1,000 event names per app, EU consent before SDK data; installs measured with the SDK alone, later widened to partners and the Conversions API.
- October 5, 2023: iOS 14+ capped at 9 campaigns per app, 9 ad accounts, 5 ad sets, 8 AEM events, no deferred deep links, no cost cap; superseded by 24 campaigns, deferred deep links from SDK 18.0.3 and cost cap ad sets. November 2023: teen ads paused in the EU, the EEA and Switzerland.
- 2024: SDK 17.0.0+ on iOS 17+ ignores the ATE flag (May 2); AEM reporting to measurement partners (October 9); 15 optimizable standard events under the old "App Installs objective" name, value optimization through the API allow-listed (October 23).
- November 2026 (current help pages): AEM covers app promotion per account; renames app installs to app promotion, automated app ads to Advantage+ app campaigns, lowest cost to Highest volume, cost cap to Cost per result goal, with older names persisting on some pages.

## Checks

- App registered, ad account linked, events flowing through one channel each; at least one install in the last 28 days.
- App promotion objective, Advantage+ app campaign; targeting limited to country, language and operating system; no expectation of age, gender or audience targeting.
- No two campaigns on one country or one audience; campaigns per app at or under 24 (18 manual); bundle ID and App Store IDs settled before launch.
- Attribution method chosen deliberately; AEM eligibility passed; SKAdNetwork configured only if a cross-platform view is needed; SDK at 17.0.0+ (18.0.3+ for deferred deep links).
- Goal matches data: event optimization only on a standard event with more than 100 attributed conversions in two weeks, completed by 1% to 30% of users; value only with purchase values and a 3-week test.
- Highest volume without a cap unless a cost target is known; budget for about 50 results a week and 88 installs a day.
- No edits or verdicts during learning; "Learning limited" fixed by combining or raising budget.
- Maximum creative uploaded per placement; refresh cadence set outside learning.
- Reports read in Ads Manager with the window stated; partner windows aligned; AEM and SKAdNetwork never compared line for line; nothing summed across iOS 14+ and other campaigns; App Store Connect counts kept separate.

## Open questions

- Whether manual setup still exists for iOS 14.5+ app promotion, given Meta's own split-test advice.
- Campaigns per app (24, 18 manual, or the 2023 figure of 9) and whether the 2023 cap of 8 AEM events still applies.
- Custom event optimization: possible in app promotion on two help pages, impossible on a third, unsupported by AEM for app events on a fourth.
- Whether Meta uses AdAttributionKit beyond SKAdNetwork 3.0 and 4.0, and why Meta advises against deep links for measurement.
- The teen pause in the EU, the EEA and Switzerland has no later status; per-account rollouts mean two advertisers can see different mechanics on the same date.
- Meta's thresholds (50 results a week, 88 installs a day, 100 conversions in two weeks) are operating guidance, and its $200 value example is illustrative, not outcome data.
